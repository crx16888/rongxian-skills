## Editing Patterns

- Agenda pages: prefer two or three clear numbered blocks with a bottom synthesis line.
- Chronology pages: use milestone cards or a horizontal timeline, but keep each milestone concise.
- BP logic pages: use three columns `CHANNEL / PRODUCT / SERVICE` when explaining commercial flywheel.
- Enterprise AI pages: connect abstract claim to concrete enterprise example, then to FDE/service conversion.
- Case pages: default structure is customer problem -> our service -> implementation path -> deliverables -> validation value.
